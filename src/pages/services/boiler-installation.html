What the job includes

Every installation we quote for covers the work below. Anything outside it is priced separately and agreed before we start.

  • Survey of your existing system before any price is given
  • Removal and disposal of the old boiler
  • Chemical flush of the system to clear debris
  • Magnetic filter fitted to protect the new unit
  • Commissioning, controls set up, and a walkthrough of how it works
  • Building Regulations notification and manufacturer warranty registration

Which boiler suits your home

There are three common types. Which one fits depends on your water pressure, how many bathrooms you have, and how much space you can give up.

Combi

Heats water on demand, so there is no cylinder and no tank in the loft. Suits smaller homes with one bathroom. Running two showers at once will drop the flow.

System

Feeds a hot water cylinder, so several outlets can run at once. Needs airing-cupboard space for the cylinder, but no loft tank. The usual choice for homes with two or more bathrooms.

Regular (heat only)

Works with both a cylinder and a feed tank in the loft. Normally kept when an older property has pipework or water pressure that would make a change expensive.

We will tell you which of these your home can take during the survey, and say plainly when the cheaper option is the right one.

How the day runs

A straight swap is usually quicker than a system change, which involves new pipework. We confirm the expected duration in writing before the work is booked — TODO-INSTALL-DURATION.

  1. We protect floors and isolate the gas and water supply.
  2. The old boiler is drained, disconnected and removed.
  3. Pipework is adjusted and the system is flushed.
  4. The new unit is fitted, filled and pressure tested.
  5. We commission it, set the controls and hand over the paperwork.

Warranty and aftercare

Manufacturer warranties on a new boiler typically depend on the unit you choose and on the boiler being serviced annually. We register the warranty for you on the day.

Do I need to be home for the whole installation?

Someone over 18 needs to be there at the start and at handover, so we can access the supply and show you the controls. You do not have to stay for the middle of the job.

Will I be without heating or hot water?

Yes, for the working part of the installation — the supply has to be isolated. On a same-day swap you normally have heating back by the evening.

Do you handle Building Regulations?

Yes. A boiler installation is notifiable work. As a Gas Safe registered business we notify it and you receive the certificate. Keep it — you will need it when you sell.
