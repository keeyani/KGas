Faults we see most

Most callouts come down to a handful of causes. Some you can check yourself before booking; the rest need an engineer.

Pressure keeps dropping

A gauge below 1 bar usually means a leak somewhere in the system, or an expansion vessel that has lost its charge. Topping it up repeatedly treats the symptom — if you are refilling more than once or twice a year, something is leaking.

Heating works but no hot water

On a combi this normally points at the diverter valve or the plate heat exchanger. On a system boiler it is more often the cylinder thermostat or a motorised valve.

An error code on the display

Codes are manufacturer-specific. Note the exact code before you reset — it is the fastest route to the right part, and a reset clears it from the display.

Banging, gurgling or whistling

Usually sludge or trapped air restricting flow, sometimes kettling in the heat exchanger from limescale. Left alone, it shortens the life of the boiler.

Visible leaks or dripping

Switch the boiler off and turn off the water supply before booking. Water and electrics are a bad combination, and a small drip can become a failed pump.

What a repair visit involves

  • Fault diagnosis, including gas pressure and flue checks
  • A price for the repair before any parts are fitted
  • Common parts carried on the van where possible
  • A safety check before we leave, and the system left working
  • An honest answer when replacing the boiler is the better value

When to stop and call immediately

Some faults are not a booking. If you smell gas, suspect carbon monoxide, or your carbon monoxide alarm sounds:

  1. Open windows and doors, and do not use switches or naked flames.
  2. Turn the gas off at the meter if you can reach it safely.
  3. Leave the property.
  4. Call the National Gas Emergency Service on 0800 111 999 — free, 24 hours.

That number is the correct first call, not ours. Once the supply is made safe we can look at the boiler — see emergency callout.

Repair or replace

Repair is usually the right answer on a boiler under about ten years old with an available part. Replacement starts to make sense when parts are obsolete, when the same fault returns, or when the repair cost approaches a meaningful share of a new unit.

We will say which we think applies and why — see boiler installation.

Common questions

What does a repair cost?

Diagnosis and labour are charged separately from parts, and we price the repair before fitting anything. Diagnosis and the first hour is £75; parts are quoted before they are fitted.

See how our pricing works.

How soon can someone come out?

We aim to attend within 24 hours of a booking, and the same day where we can.

Should I top the pressure up before you arrive?

If you know how to and the gauge is below 1 bar, yes — it may get the heating running meanwhile. Tell the engineer how often you have been refilling, because that is the useful diagnostic, not the pressure itself.

Do you repair every make of boiler?

We work on all the major domestic brands, including Worcester Bosch, Vaillant, Ideal, Baxi, Glow-worm and Viessmann.

Is the repair guaranteed?

Repairs are guaranteed for 12 months on our labour, with the manufacturer warranty on any part we supply.
