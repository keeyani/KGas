Annual boiler servicing

A yearly check that the boiler is burning safely, running efficiently, and not about to fail in the middle of winter.

Book a service Call {{site.phone.display}}

Why it is worth doing

A service is not a formality. It is the one point in the year when someone checks that the boiler is burning gas completely, that the products of combustion are going outside, and that the safety devices still do what they are there to do.

It also catches the small faults — a tired seal, a sticking valve, a pump beginning to labour — while they are still small and before they become a failure in the middle of the heating season.

If your boiler is still within its manufacturer warranty, an annual service by a Gas Safe registered engineer is usually a condition of keeping that warranty valid. Check the terms that came with the boiler, because they vary between manufacturers.

What the service covers

  • Combustion analysis against the manufacturer figures
  • Flue inspection, inside and at the terminal outside
  • Case seals checked, burner and heat exchanger cleaned where needed
  • Gas pressure and gas rate measured at the appliance
  • Safety devices tested, including the flame supervision device
  • System pressure, expansion vessel charge and controls checked
  • A written record of the readings and of anything found

Where a manufacturer specifies a different procedure for a particular model, that takes precedence over the list above.

How long it takes

A standard service takes about an hour. Allow longer if the boiler has not been serviced for several years.

Please make sure the boiler is accessible and that we can reach the flue terminal outside. If the boiler is in a loft, we will need safe access to it.

Servicing for landlords

If you let the property, you have a separate legal duty: every gas appliance and flue must be checked for safety every twelve months by a Gas Safe registered engineer, and you must give your tenant a copy of the record.

That check is not the same thing as a boiler service, though the two are commonly done on one visit. We do both, and most landlords book them together on one visit.

If something is wrong

Find a fault and you get a price before any work beyond the service begins. If the appliance is unsafe, it is classified under the industry danger categories, turned off and labelled, and you keep the paperwork explaining what was found. That is a safety requirement rather than a sales decision.

Where a repair is needed, see boiler repair. Where the boiler is near the end of its life we will say so — see boiler installation.

Is a boiler service the same as a gas safety certificate?

No. A service is maintenance of the appliance itself. A gas safety record — which landlords are legally required to hold for rented property — is a separate check of every gas appliance and the installation pipework. They are often done on the same visit, but they are not the same document.

When is the best time of year to book?

Late summer or early autumn. The boiler has been idle for months, and any fault shows up while you can still wait for a part rather than during the first cold week of the year.

What happens if the engineer finds something unsafe?

The appliance is classified according to the industry danger categories. An appliance found to be unsafe is turned off and labelled, and you are given the paperwork explaining what was found and what has to happen before it can be used again.
