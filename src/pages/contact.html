What to have ready

None of this is essential, but each one tends to turn two calls into one.

  • Your postcode, so we can confirm you are in the area
  • The make of the boiler, and the model if it is on the front
  • Any error code on the display — note it before you reset
  • What the fault is doing, and when it started
  • For a landlord check, how many gas appliances are in the property
