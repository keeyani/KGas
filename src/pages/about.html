Who we are

KGas has been carrying out domestic gas and plumbing work across Birmingham and the West Midlands since 2014. We are a small firm, which means the person who quotes the job is usually the person who turns up to do it.

We have four Gas Safe registered engineers and two apprentices.

How we work

The things below are commitments about conduct rather than claims about performance, which is why they are stated here and the numbers are not.

  • A price before the work, not after it
  • An honest answer when repair is better value than replacement, and when it is not
  • Work stops and you are consulted if the job changes
  • The paperwork left with you, not posted on at some later date
  • The property left as we found it

Registration and insurance

Anyone working on gas appliances in the United Kingdom must be on the Gas Safe Register, and must carry an ID card showing which types of work they are qualified for. You are entitled to ask to see it, and a competent engineer will expect you to.

Our registration number is {{site.gasSafe}}. You can check any registration, including ours, on the Gas Safe Register.

Insurance: {{site.insurance}}

We are members of the Chartered Institute of Plumbing and Heating Engineering, and approved installers for Worcester Bosch and Vaillant.

Qualifications

Our engineers hold ACS qualifications covering domestic natural gas: boilers, cookers, fires and meters. Several also hold unvented hot water and water regulations qualifications.

Gas qualifications are specific rather than general — being registered for boilers is not the same as being registered for cookers or commercial work. If you are unsure whether a job is one we can take, ask and we will tell you plainly.

Where we work

Complaints

If something is wrong, tell us first and give us the chance to put it right. Call or email and ask for the job to be reviewed. We will respond within five working days and put right anything that is our fault at our cost.

Concerns about the safety of gas work can also be raised directly with the Gas Safe Register, which investigates reports about registered engineers.
